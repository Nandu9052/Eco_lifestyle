# India.gov.in — National Portal of India

The National Portal of India (https://www.india.gov.in/) is designed, developed, and maintained by the National Informatics Centre (NIC), MeitY, serving as the definitive single-entry digital portal to access information and public services provided by the Union Government and state administrations.

## Core Portal Dimensions

### 1. Citizen Services Access Point
- Direct digital links to government services for:
  - Identity & Civil Documents (Aadhaar, Voter ID, PAN, Passports, Driving Licenses)
  - Education & Learning (Scholarships, CBSE, UGC, AICTE, Entrance Exam Results)
  - Agriculture & Rural Development (Kisan services, Mandi prices, Soil testing, Subsidies)
  - Jobs & Employment (UPSC, SSC, State PSCs, Employment Exchanges)
  - Health & Family Welfare (Ayushman Bharat, Immunization, Tele-medicine)
  - Energy & Sustainability (Rooftop solar applications, BEE energy standards, EV initiatives)

### 2. Government Hierarchy & Directory
- Comprehensive roster of all Central Ministries, Departments, Statutory Commissions, State Governments, Union Territory Administrations, and District Collectorates.
- Direct statutory links to legislative Acts, Government Gazette notifications, and policy white papers.

### 3. Verification & Authenticity Assurance
- Eliminates counterfeit and deceptive websites by maintaining verified official URLs (*.gov.in and *.nic.in domains only).
- Provides citizen feedback and grievance submission redirection via CPGRAMS (Centralized Public Grievance Redress and Monitoring System).

## Usage Guide
- Access https://www.india.gov.in/ to search for any government ministry, welfare programme, citizen utility, or statutory guideline.
