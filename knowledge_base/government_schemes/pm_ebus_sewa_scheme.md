# PM-eBus Sewa Scheme

The PM-eBus Sewa scheme is an ambitious initiative by the Government of India to augment city bus operations by deploying 10,000 zero-emission electric buses (e-buses) across urban centres that lack organized public transportation systems.

## Scheme Segments & Objectives
The scheme operates under two major pillars:
1. **Pillar 1 (City Bus Operations)**: Deployment of 10,000 electric buses on Public-Private Partnership (PPP) model across cities with a population between 3 lakhs and 40 lakhs (including capital cities of Union Territories and North Eastern and Hill states).
2. **Pillar 2 (Green Urban Mobility Initiatives - GUMI)**: Supporting infrastructure including behind-the-meter power substations, depot infrastructure, fast charging stations, and National Common Mobility Card (NCMC) compliant Intelligent Transport Systems (ITS).

## Target Group & Who It Is For
- Urban commuters, daily office workers, students, and city residents seeking affordable, air-conditioned, zero-tailpipe emission public transport.
- Municipal corporations and state transport undertakings (STUs) receiving central operational viability gap support.

## Verified Benefits & Subsidies
- **Operational Support**: The Central Government provides per-kilometre operational subsidies for up to 10 years to ensure fare affordability and route viability.
- **Emission Reduction**: Displaces diesel-powered city transit, preventing an estimated 16.5 million tonnes of greenhouse gas emissions over operational life.
- **Grid Integration**: Power infrastructure built under the scheme supports future electric vehicle (EV) charging networks in participating municipal zones.

## How Cities and Commuters Benefit
1. Commuters access modern electric buses equipped with automated fare collection (NCMC smart cards / UPI), real-time GPS passenger information systems, and disabled-friendly low-floor access.
2. Implemented through respective State Transport Departments and Municipal Corporations in partnership with central convergence agencies.
