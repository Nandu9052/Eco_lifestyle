# Namami Gange Mission & National Water Mission

The Ministry of Jal Shakti oversees integrated initiatives to safeguard India's freshwater resources, preserve major river basins, and promote decentralized rainwater harvesting across rural and urban settlements.

## 1. Namami Gange Mission (Integrated Ganga Conservation Programme)
- **Administering Authority**: National Mission for Clean Ganga (NMCG), Ministry of Jal Shakti.
- **Key Interventions**:
  - **Sewage Infrastructure**: Construction and modernization of high-capacity Sewage Treatment Plants (STPs) operating on Hybrid Annuity Models (HAM) to achieve zero untreated municipal discharge.
  - **Industrial Effluent Monitoring**: Real-time online effluent monitoring systems installed in grossly polluting industrial units along the Ganga and its tributaries.
  - **Rural Sanitation**: Ensuring Open Defecation Free (ODF) status across over 4,400 villages along the riverbanks, paired with liquid waste treatment and bio-digesters.
  - **Ecological Flow & Biodiversity Conservation**: Protection of aquatic keystone species including the Gangetic Dolphin, river otters, and indigenous fish species.

## 2. National Water Mission: "Jal Shakti Abhiyan: Catch the Rain"
- **Theme**: *"Catch the Rain, where it falls, when it falls."*
- **Key Components**:
  - Compulsory creation of Rainwater Harvesting Structures (RWHS) in institutional, commercial, and residential buildings.
  - Geo-tagging and rejuvenation of traditional water bodies (stepwells, tanks, and village ponds) under Mission Amrit Sarovar.
  - Afforestation along river catchments to increase soil moisture retention and prevent erosion.

## Citizen Practical Actions
1. Install rooftop rainwater catchment pipes directing runoff into groundwater recharge percolation pits.
2. Adopt low-flow aeration faucets and dual-flush cisterns at home to reduce daily domestic consumption.
3. Verify local groundwater recharge norms with your municipality or check guidelines on https://jalshakti.gov.in/.
