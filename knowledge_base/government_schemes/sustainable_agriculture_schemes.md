# Sustainable Agriculture & Farmer Empowerment Initiatives

The Ministry of Agriculture and Farmers Welfare implements targeted programs designed to modernize farming practices, lower input costs, conserve groundwater, and ensure social security for rural producers.

## Key Flagship Programs

### 1. Per Drop More Crop (PDMC)
- **Focus**: Micro-irrigation technologies (Drip and Sprinkler systems) under the Pradhan Mantri Krishi Sinchayee Yojana (PMKSY).
- **Subsidies**: Up to **55% financial assistance** for Small and Marginal farmers, and **45%** for other farmers on installation of micro-irrigation equipment.
- **Impact**: Reduces water consumption by 30–50%, saves electricity, and increases crop yields by 20–40%.

### 2. Paramparagat Krishi Vikas Yojana (PKVY)
- **Focus**: Organic farming adoption through Participatory Guarantee System (PGS) certification in clusters of 20 hectares.
- **Financial Assistance**: ₹50,000 per hectare over 3 years, of which ₹31,000 is directly provided for organic inputs (bio-fertilizers, vermicompost, bio-pesticides).

### 3. Soil Health Card Scheme
- **Focus**: Scientific soil nutrient testing across 12 parameters (N, P, K, S, Zn, Fe, Cu, Mn, Bo, pH, EC, OC).
- **Benefit**: Issued free of cost to farmers every 2 years, preventing soil degradation from over-use of chemical fertilizers.

### 4. Agriculture Infrastructure Fund (AIF)
- **Financing Facility**: ₹1 Lakh Crore fund providing medium-to-long term debt financing for post-harvest management infrastructure (warehouses, cold chains, assaying units) and community farming assets.
- **Benefit**: **3% per annum interest subvention** on loans up to ₹2 Crores for a maximum of 7 years, with credit guarantee coverage under CGTMSE.

### 5. Pradhan Mantri Kisan Maandhan Yojana (PM-KMY)
- **Pension**: Voluntary and contributory pension scheme providing **₹3,000 monthly assured pension** after reaching 60 years of age for small and marginal farmers (owning ≤ 2 hectares).
- **Contribution**: Monthly contribution between ₹55 to ₹200 (matched equally by Central Government).

## Official Verification
- Farmers can explore and apply through their district Agriculture Office, Common Service Centers (CSCs), or via https://agricoop.nic.in/.
