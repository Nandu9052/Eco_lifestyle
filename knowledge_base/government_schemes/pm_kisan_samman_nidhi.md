# PM-KISAN Samman Nidhi (Pradhan Mantri Kisan Samman Nidhi)

PM-KISAN is a Central Sector Scheme launched by the Government of India to supplement the financial needs of landholding farmer families across the country in procuring various agricultural inputs and supporting domestic requirements.

## Scheme Overview & Benefits
- **Direct Financial Assistance**: Eligible farmer families receive **₹6,000 per year**, disbursed directly into their Aadhaar-linked bank accounts via Direct Benefit Transfer (DBT).
- **Disbursement Schedule**: The amount is credited in **three equal installments of ₹2,000 every four months** (Period 1: April–July, Period 2: August–November, Period 3: December–March).
- **100% Central Funding**: Entire operational and subsidy funds are provided by the Government of India.

## Target Beneficiaries & Who It Is For
- Small and marginal farmer families as well as all landholding farmer families who own cultivable land in their names, subject to statutory exclusion criteria.
- A "family" is defined as a husband, wife, and minor children.

## Eligibility & Statutory Exclusions
The following categories are **excluded** from receiving PM-KISAN benefits:
1. **Institutional Landholders**: Agricultural lands held by institutions or corporations.
2. **Constitutional & Political Posts**: Former and present holders of constitutional posts, ministers, MPs, MLAs, MLCs, and mayors.
3. **Government Employees & Pensioners**: Serving or retired officers and employees of central/state government ministries, departments, and public sector undertakings (except multi-tasking staff / Class IV / Group D employees). Any pensioner with monthly pension of ₹10,000 or more.
4. **Income Tax Payers**: Any individual who paid income tax in the last assessment year.
5. **Professionals**: Doctors, engineers, lawyers, chartered accountants, and architects registered with professional bodies and carrying out practice.

## How Farmers Can Apply & Access Services
Farmers can access official services directly through the PM-KISAN portal (**https://pmkisan.gov.in/**) or through Common Service Centers (CSC) / State Agriculture Nodal Officers:
1. **New Farmer Registration**: Self-registration online using Aadhaar number, mobile number, state/district, and land record (khata/khasra/survey number) details.
2. **Mandatory eKYC**: Beneficiaries must complete eKYC verification via OTP on the PM-KISAN portal or biometric authentication at CSC centers.
3. **Know Your Status**: Farmers can check their registration, PFMS bank acceptance, land seeding status, and installment credit records using their Registration Number or Mobile Number.
4. **Name Correction as per Aadhaar**: Self-service correction facility available for mismatch between bank/portal records and Aadhaar details.
