# Prime Minister's Internship Scheme (PM Internship Scheme)

The Prime Minister's Internship Scheme is a pioneering initiative announced by the Government of India and implemented through the Ministry of Corporate Affairs (MCA) to offer practical corporate work experience and enhance employability for young citizens across the country.

## Scheme Overview & Key Benefits
- **Duration**: 12 months (one full year) of hands-on workplace training in top 500 partner companies across diverse industrial and technological sectors.
- **Monthly Financial Assistance**: Interns receive a **monthly stipend of ₹5,000**.
  - ₹4,500 disbursed directly by the Government of India via Direct Benefit Transfer (DBT) into the candidate's Aadhaar-linked bank account.
  - ₹500 contributed by the host company from its Corporate Social Responsibility (CSR) funds.
- **One-Time Grant**: Additional **one-time assistance of ₹6,000** provided by the Government upon joining to cover incidental and relocation expenses.
- **Insurance Coverage**: Insurance under Pradhan Mantri Jeevan Jyoti Bima Yojana and Pradhan Mantri Suraksha Bima Yojana provided by the government.

## Target Beneficiaries & Who It Is For
- Indian youth seeking real-world industry exposure, professional mentoring, and career advancement in corporate environments.
- Open to candidates from all districts across India, with special emphasis on bridging tier-2, tier-3, and rural talent into the formal economic mainstream.

## Official Eligibility Criteria
1. **Age Requirement**: Candidate must be between **21 and 24 years of age** as of the application cut-off date.
2. **Educational Qualifications**: Candidates who have passed High School (10th), Higher Secondary (12th), ITI certificate, Polytechnic diploma, or Graduate degrees (BA, B.Sc, B.Com, BCA, BBA, B.Pharma, etc.).
3. **Exclusion Criteria**:
   - Graduates from premier institutions (IITs, IIMs, IISc, NLU, etc.) or possessing professional postgraduate qualifications (CA, CMA, CS, MBBS, BDS, MBA, Master's degree) are **not eligible**.
   - Individuals currently engaged in full-time employment or full-time education are **not eligible**.
   - Anyone whose family member (self, parents, spouse) is a permanent government employee or has an annual taxable family income exceeding ₹8 Lakhs for the financial year is excluded.

## How to Apply on the Official Portal
1. **Registration**: Candidate creates an account on the central government portal: **https://pminternship.mca.gov.in/**.
2. **Profile & Resume Submission**: Fill in educational credentials, preferred sectors, skills, and geographic preferences (up to 5 internship opportunities can be selected).
3. **Shortlisting & Offer**: Automated shortlisting ensures equitable representation across districts; companies review shortlisted profiles and issue internship offers via the portal.
