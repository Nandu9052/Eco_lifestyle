# Government Schemes for Startups, MSMEs & Micro-Entrepreneurs

The Government of India, through the Ministry of Micro, Small & Medium Enterprises (MSME) and the Department for Promotion of Industry and Internal Trade (DPIIT), provides collateral-free loans, seed capital, and structural incentives to stimulate domestic enterprise, innovation, and employment.

## Key Enterprise Support Programs

### 1. Startup India Initiative
- **Official Portal**: https://www.startupindia.gov.in/
- **Benefits**:
  - **Tax Exemption**: 3-year income tax exemption under Section 80-IAC for eligible recognized startups.
  - **Seed Fund Scheme (SISFS)**: Financial assistance up to ₹20 Lakhs as grant for proof of concept and up to ₹50 Lakhs through convertible debentures or debt for commercialization.
  - **Patent & IP Fast-Tracking**: 80% rebate in patent filing fees and 50% rebate in trademark filing fees.

### 2. Pradhan Mantri MUDRA Yojana (PMMY)
- **Objective**: Refinance institutional micro-loans for non-corporate, non-farm small/micro enterprises.
- **Loan Categories**:
  - **Shishu**: Loans up to **₹50,000** for micro-businesses and early setup.
  - **Kishore**: Loans above **₹50,000 and up to ₹5,00,000** for expansion.
  - **Tarun**: Loans above **₹5,00,000 and up to ₹10,00,000** (enhanced to ₹20 Lakhs for established borrowers).
- **Features**: Collateral-free credit with MUDRA debit card facility.

### 3. Stand-Up India Scheme
- **Target**: Bank loans between **₹10 Lakhs and ₹1 Crore** to at least one Scheduled Caste (SC) or Scheduled Tribe (ST) borrower and at least one Woman borrower per bank branch for setting up greenfield enterprises.

### 4. PM SVANidhi (Street Vendor's AtmaNirbhar Nidhi)
- Micro-credit for urban vendors: Initial working capital loan of ₹10,000, progressing to ₹20,000 and ₹50,000 with 7% interest subsidy on timely repayment.

### 5. PM Vishwakarma Scheme
- Comprehensive support for traditional artisans and craftspeople across 18 trades (carpenter, blacksmith, potter, etc.).
- Subsidized collateral-free credit (₹1 Lakh in 1st tranche, ₹2 Lakhs in 2nd tranche at 5% interest), ₹15,000 toolkit e-voucher, and skill training with ₹500/day stipend.

## How to Apply
- Startups register on https://www.startupindia.gov.in/.
- MUDRA and MSME loans can be applied via the **Udyam / Udyam Assist Portal** and the national loan portal **Jansamarth** (https://www.jansamarth.in/).
