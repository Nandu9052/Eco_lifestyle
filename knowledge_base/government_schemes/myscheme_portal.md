# MyScheme — National Scheme Discovery & Access Platform

MyScheme (https://www.myscheme.gov.in/) is the Government of India's flagship single-window platform developed by the National e-Governance Division (NeGD) under the Ministry of Electronics & Information Technology (MeitY) to eliminate information barriers and streamline citizen access to public welfare programs.

## Key Capabilities & Features
- **Intelligent Eligibility Engine**: Citizens enter basic demographic criteria (age, gender, state of residence, caste category, occupation, income level, and disability status) to generate a personalized list of applicable schemes.
- **Multi-Sectoral Coverage**: Indexes over 2,000+ central and state government schemes across:
  - Agriculture, Rural & Environment
  - Education, Scholarships & Learning
  - Banking, Financial Services & Insurance
  - Business & Entrepreneurship
  - Health & Wellness
  - Housing & Shelter
  - Public Safety, Law & Justice
  - Skills & Employment
  - Social Welfare & Empowerment
  - Sports & Culture
  - Transport & Infrastructure
- **Transparent Criteria**: Provides direct access to statutory guidelines, necessary document checklists, application timelines, and direct links to authentic administering department portals.

## How Citizens Use MyScheme
1. **Explore Schemes**: Visit https://www.myscheme.gov.in/ and click 'Find Schemes For You'.
2. **Input Demographics**: Answer straightforward demographic questions without submitting sensitive personal data.
3. **Review Matched Schemes**: Filter schemes by central or state level, category, and benefit type (financial, subsidy, training, or in-kind).
4. **Apply Directly**: Click through to the official application portal verified by the government.
