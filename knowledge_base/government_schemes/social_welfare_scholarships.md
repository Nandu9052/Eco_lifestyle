# Central Social Welfare Scholarships & Inclusive Education Programs

The Ministry of Social Justice & Empowerment, along with the Ministry of Tribal Affairs and Ministry of Minority Affairs, implements targeted financial assistance programs to bridge educational disparities and promote higher education among underprivileged communities.

## Key Educational Schemes

### 1. Post-Matric Scholarship for SC and ST Students
- **Objective**: Provide comprehensive financial support to students belonging to Scheduled Castes and Scheduled Tribes pursuing post-matriculation or post-secondary courses.
- **Benefits**: Covers 100% of compulsory non-refundable fees charged by recognized institutions plus academic maintenance allowance directly disbursed to the student's Aadhaar-seeded bank account.
- **Eligibility**: Parent/guardian annual income must not exceed **₹2.50 Lakhs**.

### 2. PM YASASVI (Young Achievers Scholarship Award Scheme for Vibrant India)
- **Target Beneficiaries**: Meritorious students belonging to Other Backward Classes (OBC), Economically Backward Classes (EBC), and De-Notified Nomadic and Semi-Nomadic Tribes (DNT).
- **Sub-Components**:
  - **Top Class School Education**: Grants up to ₹75,000/year for Class 9 & 10, and ₹1,25,000/year for Class 11 & 12.
  - **Top Class College Education**: Full tuition fee reimbursement and living stipend in 250+ premier institutions.
  - **Income Ceiling**: Family annual income must not exceed ₹2.50 Lakhs.

### 3. Top Class Education Scheme for SC Students
- Full financial support covering tuition fees up to ₹2.00 Lakhs per annum in private institutions (actual fees in government institutions), living expenses of ₹3,000/month, computer assistance (₹45,000 one-time grant), and books allowance.

### 4. Scholarships for Students with Disabilities (Divyangjan)
- Implemented by the Department of Empowerment of Persons with Disabilities.
- Covers pre-matric, post-matric, top class, and national fellowships for students with ≥ 40% certified disability.

## Official Application Process
- All applications are processed online via the **National Scholarship Portal (NSP)**: https://scholarships.gov.in/.
- Authentication via Aadhaar or biometric eKYC is mandatory to ensure direct, unmediated benefit transfers.
