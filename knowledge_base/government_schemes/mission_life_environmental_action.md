# Mission LiFE (Lifestyle for Environment)

Mission LiFE was introduced by India at COP26 and launched globally in partnership with the United Nations and NITI Aayog as an international mass movement to mobilize individuals and communities to adopt sustainable daily lifestyles and become "Pro-Planet People" (P3).

## The 7 Core Action Themes & Verified Action Protocols
1. **Save Energy**: Setting air conditioners to 24°C standard baseline, transitioning to LED luminaires, turning off vehicle engines at traffic red lights, and adopting public transport.
2. **Save Water**: Installing low-flow tap aerators, practicing bucket bathing over showers, fixing municipal and domestic leaks, and collecting kitchen wastewater for plants.
3. **Say No to Single-Use Plastic**: Carrying reusable cloth/jute bags for groceries, using steel water bottles, and refusing disposable plastic cutlery.
4. **Reduce Waste**: Domestic segregation into wet, dry, and hazardous bins, backyard composting of food scraps, and gifting plants instead of plastic items.
5. **Adopt Sustainable Food Systems**: Cultivating kitchen gardens, buying local and seasonal agro-produce, minimizing household food waste through FIFO storage, and adopting traditional millets.
6. **Reduce E-Waste**: Repairing electronic devices where feasible, storing spent batteries separately with taped contacts, and handing obsolete gadgets strictly to authorized recyclers.
7. **Adopt Healthy Lifestyles**: Walking or cycling for distances under 2 kilometres and using stairs instead of elevators for low-rise floors.

## Target Beneficiaries & Who It Is For
- Every citizen, household, educational institution, Resident Welfare Association (RWA), and village Panchayat.

## Verified Institutional Support & Recognition
- **LiFE Pledge & Certification**: Citizens can take the official LiFE Pledge and receive a digital certificate from the Government of India via the portal.
- **Panchayat & Urban Body Integration**: Gram Panchayats and Urban Local Bodies receive specialized grants and recognition for zero-waste community adoption.
