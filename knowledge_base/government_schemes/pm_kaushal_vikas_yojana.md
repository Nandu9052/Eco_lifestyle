# Pradhan Mantri Kaushal Vikas Yojana (PMKVY 4.0)

Pradhan Mantri Kaushal Vikas Yojana (PMKVY) is the flagship skill development initiative of the Ministry of Skill Development and Entrepreneurship (MSDE), implemented through the National Skill Development Corporation (NSDC) to enable Indian youth to take up industry-relevant, future-ready vocational training.

## Scheme Components & Green Skills Track
- **Short Term Training (STT)**: Free vocational training ranging from 200 to 600 hours aligned with National Skills Qualification Framework (NSQF) levels. Includes specialized **Green Job Roles**:
  - *Suryamitra Solar PV Technician*: Installation, grid connection, and maintenance of rooftop and utility solar plants.
  - *Electric Vehicle Service Technician*: Diagnostics, battery pack handling, and electric powertrain servicing.
  - *Wastewater Treatment Plant Operator*: Municipal STP and ETP operations.
- **Recognition of Prior Learning (RPL)**: Formal assessment, certification, and bridge training for individuals with existing informal experience or traditional craftsmanship skills.
- **Special Projects**: Tailored skilling for vulnerable communities, tribal districts, and specialized industrial clusters.

## Target Beneficiaries & Who It Is For
- Unemployed youth, school/college dropouts, and job seekers aged **15 to 45 years** possessing valid Aadhaar identification and bank accounts.

## Verified Benefits & Certifications
- **100% Free Training**: Training fees, assessment costs, and certification expenses are completely sponsored by the Government of India.
- **Government Certification**: Recognized NSQF certificate with Skill India digital credential, opening doors for domestic employment and international mobility.
- **Accidental Insurance & Stipends**: Insurance coverage during training and post-placement transit allowances where applicable.

## How to Enroll & Access Courses
1. **Digital Registration**: Browse and register through the Skill India Digital Hub at **https://www.skillindia.gov.in/** or through Pradhan Mantri Kaushal Kendras (PMKK).
2. **Counseling & Selection**: Undergo automated skill aptitude profiling and choose nearest empanelled training centres and preferred vocational sectors.
