# JnanaBhumi — Andhra Pradesh Post-Matric Scholarship & Student Welfare Services

JnanaBhumi is the dedicated digital public service portal developed and maintained by the Government of Andhra Pradesh to deliver post-matric scholarships, tuition fee reimbursements, and maintenance support to eligible students in the state.

## Scheme Overview
The platform acts as a unified single-window system for students, educational institutions, and welfare departments (Social Welfare, Tribal Welfare, Backward Classes Welfare, Minorities Welfare, and Higher Education Department).

Key welfare components administered under JnanaBhumi include:
- **Reimbursement of Tuition Fee (RTF) / Jagananna Vidya Deevena**: Full fee reimbursement for post-matric courses (ITI, Polytechnic, Undergraduate Degrees, B.Tech, Medicine, MBA, MCA, and other professional courses) directly credited to mother's bank account in quarterly tranches.
- **Maintenance Support (MTF) / Jagananna Vasathi Deevena**: Annual financial assistance towards food, hostel, and accommodation expenses (₹10,000 for ITI, ₹15,000 for Polytechnic, and ₹20,000 for Degree/Professional courses per annum in two installments).

## Target Group & Who It Is For
- Post-Matric students enrolled in recognized government, aided, and private educational institutions in Andhra Pradesh.
- Caters to students from SC, ST, BC, EBC, Kapu, Minority, and Differently Abled communities who meet economic eligibility criteria.

## Official Eligibility Criteria
1. **Domicile**: Candidate must be a bonafide resident of Andhra Pradesh.
2. **Attendance**: Minimum 75% biometric/facial attendance required across the academic semester/year.
3. **Income Ceiling**: Total family annual income must not exceed ₹2.50 Lakh per annum.
4. **Landholding & Asset Rules**: Maximum wetland ownership up to 10 acres, dryland up to 25 acres (or 25 acres combined). No family member should be a permanent government employee, pensioner (sanitary workers exempted), or income-tax assessee. Four-wheeler ownership (excluding taxis/tractors/autos) is not permitted.

## How to Apply & Access Student Services
1. **Online Portal Access**: Visit the official portal at **https://jnanabhumi.ap.gov.in/**.
2. **Registration & Verification**: Students submit fresh or renewal applications with Aadhaar, ration card/rice card, SSC hall ticket, caste, and income credentials through their college or local Grama/Ward Sachivalayam.
3. **eKYC & Aadhaar Linking**: Bank account of the student's mother must be Aadhaar-seeded and NPCI-mapped for Direct Benefit Transfer (DBT).
4. **Know Your Status**: Students can log in to the portal using their Aadhaar/User ID to track application sanction, biometric verification, and scholarship disbursement status.
