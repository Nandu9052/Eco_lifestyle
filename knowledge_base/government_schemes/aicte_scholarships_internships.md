# AICTE Scholarships, Fellowships & National Internship Portal

The All India Council for Technical Education (AICTE), statutory body under the Department of Higher Education, Ministry of Education, Government of India, administers flagship financial support schemes and internship initiatives for technical education students across India.

## Major AICTE Scholarship Schemes

### 1. AICTE Pragati Scholarship Scheme for Girl Students
- **Objective**: Empower young women to pursue technical education (diploma and degree levels) in AICTE-approved institutions.
- **Benefits**: ₹50,000 per annum for every year of study as a lump-sum amount towards tuition fee waiver, purchase of books, equipment, laptops, software, etc.
- **Eligibility**:
  - Maximum two girl children per family are eligible.
  - Family annual income must not exceed ₹8 Lakhs from all sources during the preceding financial year.
  - Candidate must be admitted into the 1st year of Degree/Diploma course or 2nd year through lateral entry in an AICTE-approved institution.

### 2. AICTE Saksham Scholarship Scheme for Differently-Abled Students
- **Objective**: Support specially-abled youth in pursuing technical education.
- **Benefits**: ₹50,000 per annum for every year of study towards academic and living allowances.
- **Eligibility**:
  - Specially-abled students having disability of not less than 40% (certified by authorized medical authority).
  - Family annual income must be below ₹8 Lakhs.
  - Admitted to 1st year of Degree/Diploma or 2nd year lateral entry in an AICTE-approved technical institution.

### 3. AICTE Swanath Scholarship Scheme
- **Objective**: Provide financial assistance to orphan children, wards of parents who died due to COVID-19, and wards of Armed Forces/Central Paramilitary personnel martyred in action.
- **Benefits**: ₹50,000 per annum for each year of degree/diploma studies.
- **Eligibility**: Certified orphan status or martyred personnel certificate, enrolled in AICTE-approved colleges with family income ≤ ₹8 Lakhs.

## National AICTE Internship Portal
- **Official URL**: https://internship.aicte-india.org/
- **Overview**: A unified national platform connecting technical students (Engineering, Management, Pharmacy, Architecture, Applied Arts) with internships in central ministries, municipal corporations (e.g., TULIP - The Urban Learning Internship Program), public sector undertakings (PSUs), and industry partners.
- **Features**: Verified digital certificates, stipendiary industry training, and academic credit recognition under the National Credit Framework (NCrF).

## How to Apply
1. **Scholarships**: Apply online through the National Scholarship Portal (NSP) at https://scholarships.gov.in/ during the open notification window.
2. **Internships**: Register directly on https://internship.aicte-india.org/ with student credentials and institute enrollment details.
3. **Disbursement**: Scholarships are credited directly to the student's Aadhaar-seeded bank account through Direct Benefit Transfer (DBT).
