# National Scholarship Portal (NSP)

The National Scholarship Portal (NSP) is a mission-mode initiative under Digital India aimed at providing a single, simplified, and transparent electronic platform for scholarship disbursement directly to students from central and state ministries.

## Scheme Scope & Participating Departments
NSP unifies over 100 scholarship schemes offered by major Central Government bodies:
- **Ministry of Social Justice and Empowerment**: Top Class Education for SC students, Post-Matric Scholarships for Scheduled Castes.
- **Ministry of Tribal Affairs**: National Fellowship and Scholarship for Higher Education of ST students.
- **Ministry of Minority Affairs**: Pre-Matric, Post-Matric, and Merit-cum-Means Scholarships for minority communities.
- **Department of Higher Education (MoE)**: Central Sector Scheme of Scholarship for College and University Students.
- **Department of Empowerment of Persons with Disabilities**: Post-Matric and Top-Class Scholarships for Differently Abled Students.

## Target Beneficiaries & Who It Is For
- School students (Classes 1–12), college undergraduates, postgraduate scholars, doctoral researchers, and professional course trainees from economically weaker sections, marginalized categories, and meritorious backgrounds nationwide.

## Verified Eligibility Criteria
1. **Academic Standing**: Minimum qualifying marks as stipulated per individual scheme (typically 50% to 80% in previous terminal examination).
2. **Family Income Threshold**: Ranging from ₹1.50 Lakh to ₹8.00 Lakhs per annum depending on specific scheme guidelines (e.g. Merit-cum-Means: ₹2.50 Lakh, Central Sector Scheme: ₹4.50 Lakh).
3. **One Scholarship Policy**: A student can avail only one scholarship from central or state sources for a given academic course.

## How to Apply & Key Portal Features
1. **One-Time Registration (OTR)**: Students complete OTR on **https://scholarships.gov.in/** using Aadhaar / Aadhaar Enrolment ID and mobile OTP to generate a permanent reference number.
2. **Scheme Selection**: The portal dynamically maps student profile, course level, caste, and income parameters to recommend matching active schemes.
3. **Document Verification**: Multi-tier digital verification by the Head of the Institution (School/College), District Nodal Officer, and State Nodal Officer.
4. **Direct Benefit Transfer (DBT)**: Scholarship amount (course fees + maintenance allowances) transferred directly to the student's Aadhaar-seeded bank account through the Public Financial Management System (PFMS).
