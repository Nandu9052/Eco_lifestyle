# Skill India Digital & National Apprenticeship Promotion Scheme (NAPS)

Skill India Digital is the comprehensive digital public infrastructure launched by the Ministry of Skill Development and Entrepreneurship (MSDE) and National Skill Development Corporation (NSDC) to unify skill development courses, apprenticeships, and employment opportunities across India.

## Key Programmes & Pathways

### 1. National Apprenticeship Promotion Scheme (NAPS)
- **Objective**: Promote formal industry apprenticeships for ITI graduates, diploma holders, and general stream graduates.
- **Stipend Support**: Government provides financial assistance up to 25% of prescribed stipend (maximum ₹1,500 per month per apprentice) transferred directly to apprentices through DBT under NAPS-2.
- **Portal**: https://www.apprenticeshipindia.gov.in/

### 2. Pradhan Mantri Kaushal Vikas Yojana (PMKVY)
- Short-term training (STT) and Recognition of Prior Learning (RPL) across 35+ industry sectors.
- Includes **Green Skills Certifications**: Solar panel installation (Surya Mitra), EV service technician, waste water plant operation, and organic agricultural management.
- 100% government-funded training with certified NSQF (National Skills Qualifications Framework) certification.

### 3. Skill India Digital Hub (SIDH)
- Digital credential wallet hosting digitally verifiable certificates linked to DigiLocker and APAAR student IDs.
- Course offerings in Artificial Intelligence, Cloud Computing, Cyber Security, Solar Technologies, and Advanced Manufacturing.

## How to Register
- Visit https://www.skillindia.gov.in/ or download the Skill India Digital App.
- Register with mobile number and Aadhaar OTP verification to explore localized training centers and open apprenticeship contracts.
