# Andhra Pradesh Government Services, Agriculture & Sustainability Programs

The Government of Andhra Pradesh administers an extensive array of digital citizen services, agricultural welfare packages, and clean energy initiatives for residents across all 26 districts of the state.

## 1. AP MeeSeva — Unified Digital Citizen Gateway
- **Official Portal**: https://meeseva.ap.gov.in/ & Grama/Ward Sachivalayam services.
- **Services Provided**: Over 300+ government-to-citizen (G2C) and government-to-business (G2B) digital services:
  - Integrated Caste, Income, Residence, and Date of Birth certificates required for educational scholarships and welfare applications.
  - Land records and encumbrance certificates (Meebhoomi portal integration).
  - Electricity connection, water connection, and municipal trade licensing services.

## 2. Farmer Assistance Programs (Annadata Sukhibhava / Rythu Bharosa)
- **Objective**: Direct financial assistance for agricultural inputs and livelihood stability for land-owning and eligible tenant farmer families in Andhra Pradesh.
- **Financial Benefit**: Provides direct financial support (complemented with Central PM-KISAN funds) disbursed in seasonal installments ahead of Kharif and Rabi agricultural operations.
- **Free Crop Insurance**: State-funded crop insurance covering registered agricultural acreage through e-Crop booking without requiring farmer premium contribution.
- **Zero-Interest Crop Loans (Vaddi Leni Runalu)**: Interest reimbursement on crop loans repaid within the statutory tenure.

## 3. NREDCAP (New & Renewable Energy Development Corporation of Andhra Pradesh)
- **Official Portal**: https://nredcap.gov.in/
- **Key Initiatives**:
  - **Solar Agriculture Pumpsets**: Deployment of standalone and grid-connected DC/AC solar pumps with central and state subsidies.
  - **Grid-Connected Rooftop Solar**: Facilitation of net-metering solar installations across domestic households and educational institutions.
  - **Electric Mobility Support**: Rollout of public EV charging stations across AP national highways and urban centers.

## 4. Student & Youth Programs (JnanaBhumi & APSSDC)
- **Scholarships**: Administered through JnanaBhumi (https://jnanabhumi.ap.gov.in/) providing full tuition fee reimbursement and maintenance allowances.
- **Skill Training**: Andhra Pradesh State Skill Development Corporation (APSSDC - https://apssdc.in/) offers certified vocational training, coding bootcamps, and placement drives for youth.
