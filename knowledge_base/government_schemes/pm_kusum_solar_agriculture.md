# PM-KUSUM Scheme (Pradhan Mantri Kisan Urja Suraksha evam Utthaan Mahabhiyan)

PM-KUSUM is a flagship initiative of the Ministry of New and Renewable Energy (MNRE), Government of India, designed to de-dieselise the agricultural sector, provide energy security to farmers, and generate additional rural income through clean solar power generation.

## Three Key Scheme Components
1. **Component A (Grid-Connected Solar Plants on Barren Land)**: Individual farmers, farmer producer organizations (FPOs), or panchayats can install small solar power plants (500 kW to 2 MW capacity) on barren, fallow, or agricultural land within 5 km of an electricity substation. Power is sold to the local DISCOM under long-term 25-year Power Purchase Agreements (PPAs).
2. **Component B (Standalone Solar Agriculture Pumps)**: Individual farmers in off-grid areas receive massive financial subsidies to replace diesel water pumps with solar-powered irrigation pumps (up to 7.5 HP capacity).
3. **Component C (Solarisation of Grid-Connected Agriculture Pumps)**: Solarizing existing agricultural grid-connected pump sets, allowing farmers to irrigate fields using solar power and sell surplus generated electricity back to DISCOMs at pre-fixed tariffs.

## Target Beneficiaries & Who It Is For
- Individual farmers, groups of farmers, cooperatives, Panchayats, Water User Associations (WUAs), and Farmer Producer Organizations (FPOs) across rural India.

## Verified Financial Subsidies & Benefits
- **Up to 60% Capital Subsidy**: Under Component B and Component C, the Government provides substantial financial support:
  - **30% Central Financial Assistance (CFA)** from MNRE.
  - **30% State Subsidy** from the respective State Government.
  - **30% Bank Loan**: Facilitated through priority sector lending.
  - **10% Farmer Share**: The farmer contributes only 10% of the total system cost upfront.
- In North Eastern states, Sikkim, Jammu & Kashmir, Ladakh, Himachal Pradesh, Uttarakhand, and island territories, the Central Government CFA is increased to **50%**, reducing farmer contribution further.

## How to Apply
1. Farmers apply through their designated State Nodal Agency (SNA) or State Renewable Energy Development Corporation (e.g. NREDCAP in Andhra Pradesh, MEDA in Maharashtra, KREDL in Karnataka).
2. Application tracking, approved vendor empanelment, and capacity allocations are managed through the central PM-KUSUM portal: **https://pmkusum.mnre.gov.in/**.
3. Beware of fraudulent websites: PM-KUSUM does not collect registration fees on unauthorized private websites; apply only through official state nodal agencies.
