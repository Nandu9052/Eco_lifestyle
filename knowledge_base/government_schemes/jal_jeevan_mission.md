# Jal Jeevan Mission & Mission Amrit Sarovar

The Jal Jeevan Mission (JJM), spearheaded by the Ministry of Jal Shakti, Government of India, is dedicated to providing functional household tap connections (FHTC) with safe, potable water (55 litres per capita per day) to every rural household across India by promoting community-managed water infrastructure and recharge mechanisms.

## Key Program Pillars
1. **Har Ghar Jal Tap Infrastructure**: In-village water supply infrastructure, bulk water transfer systems, and automated IoT-based smart water quality monitoring sensors.
2. **Source Sustainability & Graywater Management**: Mandatory inclusion of groundwater recharge structures, rainwater harvesting systems in schools and public buildings, and soak pits for graywater treatment.
3. **Mission Amrit Sarovar**: Targeted program to construct and rejuvenate at least 75 water bodies (ponds, tanks, reservoirs) in every rural district of India to arrest groundwater depletion and harvest surface runoff.

## Target Beneficiaries & Who It Is For
- Rural households, village panchayats, Paani Samitis, Anganwadi centres, and primary health clinics across India.

## Verified Household Benefits & Community Governance
- **Potable Tap Water**: Eradicates the drudgery of fetching water from distant unverified sources, mitigating waterborne fluorosis, arsenic toxicity, and bacterial infections.
- **Village Paani Samitis**: At least 50% women representation mandated in village water and sanitation committees (VWSC) for local operation, maintenance, and tariff collection.
- **Water Quality Testing**: Field Test Kits (FTKs) provided to five nominated women in each village for on-spot bacteriological and chemical testing.

## How to Access & Verify Water Services
1. Village residents participate in Gram Sabha water planning sessions to prepare Village Action Plans (VAPs).
2. Water quality data and coverage statistics are publicly accessible in real time via the JJM Dashboard at **https://jaljeevanmission.gov.in/**.
