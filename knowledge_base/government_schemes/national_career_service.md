# National Career Service (NCS)

The National Career Service (NCS) is a Mission Mode Project implemented by the Ministry of Labour and Employment, Government of India, establishing a digital nationwide platform to bridge job seekers, students, and employers through transparent, verified public career services.

## Key Services Provided
1. **Job Matching & Listings**: Thousands of verified active vacancies spanning Central and State Government departments, Public Sector Undertakings (PSUs), and authenticated private enterprises.
2. **Internships & Apprenticeships**: Dedicated portal segment listing verified internships for college students and recent graduates across public infrastructure, banking, manufacturing, and IT sectors.
3. **Free Career Counselling**: Access to certified career counsellors across 1,000+ Model Career Centers (MCCs) in districts nationwide.
4. **Vocational & Skill Assessment**: Integrated skill assessments, psychometric tests, and direct links to Skill India digital certification courses.
5. **Specialized Job Fairs**: Regular virtual and offline district-level Rozgar Melas providing spot interviews and appointment offers.

## Eligibility & Registration
- Open to all Indian citizens aged 18 and above, including 10th/12th pass students, diploma holders, graduates, and professionals.
- Candidates register for free at https://www.ncs.gov.in/ using their Aadhaar or recognized national identity number to receive an authenticated NCS ID.
- No intermediary charges or registration fees: all NCS services are 100% free of cost provided by the Union Government.
