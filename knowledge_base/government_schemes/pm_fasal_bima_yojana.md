# Pradhan Mantri Fasal Bima Yojana (PMFBY)

The Pradhan Mantri Fasal Bima Yojana (PMFBY), administered by the Department of Agriculture & Farmers Welfare, Ministry of Agriculture, is India's flagship crop insurance scheme ensuring income stability for farming families against adverse weather and natural calamities.

## Coverage & Risk Stages
1. **Prevented Sowing/Planting Risk**: Coverage when insured farmers are unable to sow due to deficit rainfall or adverse seasonal conditions.
2. **Standing Crop Loss**: Comprehensive yield loss coverage from sowing to harvesting caused by non-preventable natural risks (drought, flood, unseasonal rains, landslides, hailstorms, cyclones, and pest infestations).
3. **Post-Harvest Losses**: Coverage for up to 14 days after harvesting for crops left in 'cut and spread' condition in the field against cyclone, cyclonic rains, and unseasonal rainfall.
4. **Localized Calamities**: Individual farm-level assessments for losses due to hailstorm, landslide, cloudburst, and inundation.

## Minimal Farmer Premium Share
- **Kharif Food & Oilseed Crops**: Maximum **2.0%** of sum insured.
- **Rabi Food & Oilseed Crops**: Maximum **1.5%** of sum insured.
- **Commercial / Horticultural Crops**: Maximum **5.0%** of sum insured.
- The entire remaining actuarial premium subsidy is shared equally (50:50) between the Government of India and the respective State Government.

## Claim Settlement & Technology
- Integrated with satellite remote sensing, automated weather stations, and the National Crop Insurance Portal (NCIP).
- Claims are credited directly via DBT into the farmer's bank account linked to the insurance policy.

## How to Enroll
- Enrolled automatically through Kisan Credit Card (KCC) loans, or voluntarily through Common Service Centers (CSCs), bank branches, and the official portal at https://pmfby.gov.in/.
