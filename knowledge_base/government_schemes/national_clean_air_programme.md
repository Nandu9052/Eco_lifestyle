# National Clean Air Programme (NCAP)

The National Clean Air Programme (NCAP), launched by the Ministry of Environment, Forest and Climate Change (MoEFCC), is a time-bound national strategy to prevent, control, and abate air pollution across 131 targeted non-attainment cities across India.

## Objectives & Target Reductions
- Target reduction of **20% to 30% in PM2.5 and PM10 concentrations** compared to baseline levels, updated to 40% reduction targets for select major metropolitan centers by 2026.
- Expansion of real-time ambient air quality monitoring networks under the Continuous Ambient Air Quality Monitoring Stations (CAAQMS) system.

## Action Pillars Across Key Sectors
1. **Vehicular Emissions Control**:
   - Mandating Bharat Stage VI (BS-VI) fuel and vehicle standards nationwide.
   - Promoting Electric Vehicles (EVs) through municipal fleet transition, EV bus deployment, and public charging networks.
   - Phasing out commercial diesel vehicles older than 15 years in polluted urban clusters.
2. **Industrial Pollution Abatement**:
   - Compulsory retrofitting of flue-gas desulfurization (FGD) systems in thermal power stations.
   - Transitioning industrial units in NCR and major urban areas to piped natural gas (PNG) and clean fuels.
3. **Road Dust & Construction Waste Management**:
   - Mechanized sweeping of urban arterial roads and water-sprinkling mist cannons.
   - Enforcement of Construction & Demolition (C&D) Waste Management Rules requiring wind-breaking curtains and enclosed debris transport.
4. **Biomass & Waste Burning Control**:
   - Zero-tolerance enforcement against municipal open-waste burning.
   - Ex-situ and in-situ management of agricultural crop residue (happy seeders, bio-decomposer solutions).

## Citizen Transparency & Monitoring Portal: PRANA
- **PRANA (Portal for Regulation of Air-pollution in Non-Attainment cities)**: https://prana.cpcb.gov.in/
- Citizens can monitor live city-level air quality index (AQI) values, municipal action plans, and track central funding utilization across all 131 cities.
