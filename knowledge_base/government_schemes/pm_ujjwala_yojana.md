# Pradhan Mantri Ujjwala Yojana (PMUY 2.0)

Pradhan Mantri Ujjwala Yojana (PMUY) was launched by the Ministry of Petroleum and Natural Gas, Government of India, to provide clean cooking fuel (Liquefied Petroleum Gas - LPG) to rural and deprived households, replacing polluting traditional biomass, coal, and cow-dung cakes that cause indoor air pollution and severe respiratory health hazards for women and children.

## Scheme Features & Direct Benefits under Ujjwala 2.0
- **Deposit-Free Connection**: Complete waiver of security deposit for a 14.2 kg (or 5 kg) domestic LPG cylinder, pressure regulator, and safety hose pipe.
- **Free First Refill & Hotplate**: Beneficiary receives the first LPG cylinder refill and a domestic gas stove (hotplate) completely free of cost upon enrollment.
- **Targeted Targeted Subsidy**: Government of India provides an active per-cylinder subsidy (credited via Direct Benefit Transfer into the beneficiary's Aadhaar-linked bank account) for up to 12 refills per year.

## Target Beneficiaries & Who It Is For
- Adult women belonging to economically disadvantaged households across India.
- Priority categories include SC/ST households, beneficiaries of Pradhan Mantri Awas Yojana (Gramin), Antyodaya Anna Yojana (AAY), forest dwellers, Most Backward Classes (MBC), tea and ex-tea garden tribes, and island/river island residents.

## Official Eligibility Criteria
1. The applicant must be an **adult woman (at least 18 years of age)**.
2. There must be **no existing LPG connection** in the same household under any Oil Marketing Company (IOCL, BPCL, HPCL).
3. The household must satisfy self-declaration poverty criteria or belong to designated 14-point exclusion-free economic categories. Migrant workers require only a self-declaration for proof of address.

## How to Apply
1. **Online Application**: Apply via the unified national portal: **https://www.pmuy.gov.in/** or through respective oil company portals (Indane, Bharatgas, HP Gas).
2. **Offline Submission**: Submit the standard 2-page application along with Aadhaar, ration card/family composition document, and bank account details directly at the nearest authorized LPG distributor.
